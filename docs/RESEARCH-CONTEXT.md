## What the evidence establishes

The original build is recalled as approximately **February 27, 2026**. Related February 27 research and February 28 article-drafting conversations survive, but the Cowork build conversation and exact starting prompt do not. A source named in an AI-generated research response is evidence of research context, not proof it was read, accepted or incorporated into the missing prompt. See [build provenance](PROVENANCE.md).

This is direct evidence of prior engagement. It does not establish that the current web application existed in 2024.

## What later information justifies changing

**Prioritize interpretation and provenance improvements. This review found no verified post-build formula change that requires replacing the ratio calculations.** The following are recommendations for a future revision, not claims that these changes have been implemented.

| Priority | Later source | Recommended response | Current tool and remaining gap |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1** | [Samantha Musoke’s comment](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/i-reverse-engineered-grant-guardian-ai-tool-foundations-wayan-vota-4jfse), after Mar 4 | Capture accounting basis, reporting framework, reporting entity and units before interpreting ratios. Provide an “unknown” option and prompt for review of grant timing/restrictions. | Current v2 records documents, periods, currency, references and notes. It does not explicitly capture accounting basis, statement scope or units. Do not imply automatic conversion between reporting frameworks or require INPAS universally. |
| **2** | [Juliet MacDowell’s comment](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/i-reverse-engineered-grant-guardian-ai-tool-foundations-wayan-vota-4jfse), after Mar 4 | Label settings as illustrative, user-entered or funder-confirmed; record the source, confirmation date and intended applicability. | V2 has scenarios and assumption notes. A dedicated provenance field would make exported settings less likely to be mistaken for a funder’s actual policy. |

Before implementing the first recommendation, have a nonprofit accountant review the field definitions and examples. Preserve the entered figures and uncertainty; do not silently reinterpret statements. A useful acceptance check is that a reader can identify the accounting basis, reporting scope, period and units from the exported report, including which are unknown.

For settings provenance, a useful acceptance check is that an imported/exported scenario retains its source and date, and that the interface never labels settings “funder-confirmed” solely because the user selected that label. Supporting documentation and human confirmation are still needed.

## Dates and claims that should not trigger a rebuild

- **AImpactful is a pre-build interview.** Its page shows February 23 publication, while crawled versions expose April and September modification timestamps. Those timestamps do not establish a new product release. Its discussion of seven responsible-AI principles versus five emphasized in the [2025 product essay](https://medium.com/patrick-j-mcgovern-foundation/social-responsibility-comes-first-product-development-lessons-from-grant-guardian-3c5fe6916019) is a difference in presentation, not evidence of changed ratio calculations.
- **GitLab’s account is older than its modification stamp.** Page metadata gives June 9, 2025 publication and March 26, 2026 modification. No version comparison was available to establish a substantive post-build change. [Source](https://www.gitlabfoundation.org/our-journey/ai-in-action).
- **Adoption counts are dated and inconsistent.** Chronicle social promotions repeat figures above 130/134; its June 2025 article reports 189; the February 2026 interview reports over 420. Do not treat reposts as independent counts, refresh a live counter from them or infer efficacy from adoption. See S05, S07, S42 and S47 in the [inventory](PUBLIC-DISCUSSIONS.md).
- **A 2025 publication deposited in March 2026 is not automatically new research.** The UNIGE manuscript identifies itself as a 2025 publication and a March 12, 2026 repository update. Its product citation gives no reason to alter the analyzer. [Repository](https://archive-ouverte.unige.ch/unige:192243).
- **The NationSwell/IBM report’s release date remains unresolved.** A January filename and March upload path are insufficient. Its indexed mention is retained as S53, with lower confidence and no claim it appeared after the build.

## Clarify the public article before expanding functionality

The March article describes what a program officer would see and advises around a three-month reserve threshold. It also uses “restricted-to-unrestricted” in an example even though the documented ratio’s denominator is **total net assets**. Those statements deserve a dated clarification if the article is updated. PJMF publishes customizable examples, not a universal foundation scoring standard. [Original article](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/i-reverse-engineered-grant-guardian-ai-tool-foundations-wayan-vota-4jfse); [PJMF definitions](https://www.mcgovern.org/our-work/data-solutions/grant-guardian/).

The September application is a manual ratio explorer with an optional illustrative index. It does not upload financial documents, reproduce private funder weights, verify entered figures or predict grant decisions. These limits are documented in the [README](../README.md) and [methods](METHODS.md). Keep them visible in any revised public description. No LinkedIn article or live application was changed in this documentation review.
