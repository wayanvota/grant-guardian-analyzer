A currently accessible page can differ from its pre-build version.

## Ranking method

Editorial usefulness for documenting this build is scored, not popularity, endorsement or scientific validity. Each factor receives 0–5 points; total = 5 × (D + E + A + P), out of 100. Ties retain source-ID order; small score differences have no statistical meaning.

- **D, directness:** 5 for detailed Grant Guardian mechanics or a concrete analyzer issue; 3 for a substantive broader discussion; 1 for a passing social reference.
- **E, evidence:** 5 for inspectable primary documentation or a direct account in its relevant domain; 4 for attributable reporting/comments; 3 for partial text or secondary synthesis; 2 for an indexed excerpt. This measures traceability, not proof that the speaker’s claims are correct.
- **A, actionability:** 5 for a specific implementation/interpretation decision; 3 for useful design context; 1 for awareness only; 0 for no substantive content.
- **P, provenance:** 5 for a source named in the recovered February 27 research or directly engaged with beforehand; 4 for a direct author account of this build/experiment; 3 for related author history; 2 for pre-build availability without evidence of use; 1 for later/undated context.

“Named in recovered research” means a source appears in an AI-generated research response. It does not prove Wayan opened it, accepted its claims or put it into the missing Cowork prompt. That response contains errors, including the Chronicle publication date. Public source dates take precedence here.

The top seven are S01–S07. Critical comments remain prominent in the [update review](RESEARCH-CONTEXT.md#what-later-information-justifies-changing). A low provenance score for a later comment does not diminish its product importance.
