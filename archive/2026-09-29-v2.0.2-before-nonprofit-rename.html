03 / Financial ratio report

Your figures, in context.

The method, in plain sight

Useful questions.
No automatic verdicts.

Three ratios

Current assets ÷ current liabilities. Available unrestricted cash and liquid investments ÷ average monthly expenses. Donor-restricted net assets ÷ total net assets.

Reserve coverage assumes the entered period’s average spending is representative. It does not predict future cash flow.

Explicit assumptions

PJMF publishes examples of a 1.0 or 0.5 current-ratio threshold. Other thresholds, weights, and the optional score here are this simulator’s assumptions. You can change them and compare the result.

Missing evidence stays missing. No risk grade or funding recommendation is produced.
