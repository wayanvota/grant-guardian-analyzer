What this tool does: This simulates what a Grant Guardian report looks like, using PJMF's three publicly documented financial ratios (Current Ratio, Cash Reserve Ratio, Donor-Restricted Net Assets Ratio). Individual foundations configure their own thresholds and weights — this tool uses documented examples. Grant Guardian itself uses Claude AI to extract these numbers from uploaded PDFs; here you enter them manually. Sources: Official Product Page · PJMF Medium (Social Responsibility) · Anthropic Case Study
